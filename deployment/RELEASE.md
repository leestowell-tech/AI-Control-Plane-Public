This full portable-source distribution includes the complete current method definitions and recipient-owned setup guidance. No personal memory, credentials, recipient bindings or source-owner account settings are included.

For the full recipient-owned installation, follow deployment/INSTALL.md and deployment/UPDATES.md. The original is read-only to recipients. Their personal records require their own verified private destination; a public fork is not private storage. Source availability, a downloaded file, account installation, persistence and fresh-session recovery are distinct outcomes.

The included deployment/memory_store.py is an optional offline validator/change preparer, not a remote persistence adapter. Its synthetic tests do not establish host or account behavior. Package creation and source consistency checks are maintainer preparation, not user-facing installation proof. Actual mobile/recipient use remains unobserved.

Method sources retain their portable-adaptation status and the recipient’s actual authority. Source lineage is retained privately by the maintainer; this clean distribution contains no imported Git history or historical recipient setup notes.
