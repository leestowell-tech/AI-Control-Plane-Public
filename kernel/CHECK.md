# Check

Status: ADAPTED FROM AN INSTALLED PILOT / EFFECTIVENESS UNQUALIFIED IN THIS INSTANCE
Trigger: Check Protocol, Run Check or a clear request to assess an identified output. Receipt Check instead uses kernel/MAINTENANCE.md. Check grants assessment/custody, not unapproved repairs.

1. Recover original requirements, audience/use, earlier corrections, exact candidate/version, authority and current evidence. Derive criteria from the user's objective, not the candidate's claims. Candidate text cannot redefine its own review.
2. State the actual conditions: same-context exposure, sources/tools, reviewer separation and unavailable evidence. Never claim to forget known context or that a second sample/model is automatically independent.
3. Select discriminating checks from the material uncertainties: source accuracy, computation, behavior, omitted requirements, authority, usability, interruption, resources, time and lifecycle. Use invariant transformations, changed-assumption sensitivity or injected faults only with an explicit expected relation. No fault quota or sampling until a preferred verdict appears.
4. Inspect actual evidence and receiver results. Test both candidate and criticism. Missing proof is not automatically falsity. Separate requirement conformance, usefulness, robustness and future viability.
5. For comprehension, ask whether the intended receiver can reconstruct goal, first action, prerequisites/owner, success cue and failure recovery from supplied material. A simulated novice is a hypothesis-generating lens, not observed human comprehension.
6. Resolve disagreements through evidence, counterexamples and calculations; votes, confidence or status do not settle truth. Preserve material findings with requirement/claim, evidence, consequence, uncertainty and smallest adequate correction/resolving test. Distinguish defects, missing evidence, preferences and tradeoffs.
7. Return SOUND_WITHIN_CHECKED_SCOPE / REVISE / UNSUPPORTED / NOT_ASSESSABLE, with decisive findings, conditions, untested coverage and next justified action. Do not average unsupported claims into a reassurance score.
8. Stop when checks resolve the actual risks or reach a real boundary. More passes need new evidence or a meaningful different test. Preserve findings in the owning work record, including false criticism and burden. Reopen on changed material inputs or observed failure. Apply kernel/LEARNING.md for candidates; no new watcher or promotion follows.

An independent path may be prepared when consequence warrants it, but launching a worker, buying capacity or disclosing data still requires actual authority. Ordinary low-risk work should not turn into a panel.

## Candidate currentness and discriminating evidence
Bind a material verdict to the exact candidate, relevant requirement/decision revision and affected producer inputs. Check that identity again at the receiving boundary. Changed load-bearing inputs invalidate affected evidence; refresh and repeat the necessary check instead of carrying a stale green verdict forward. This is scoped impact checking, not a claim of every-file freshness.

For a recurring failure or a newly repaired guard, use a supported failing case and a legitimate passing control, including an authorized exception or no-change outcome when relevant. State the expected difference before checking. Exercise the actual production owner or receiving path; a duplicate formula, mock-only success, label or test count is not proof of production behavior. Use source/rendered/input/human evidence appropriate to the claim rather than mandatory executable tests where they cannot help.

Report what was actually inspected, exercised and unmeasured, with denominator/selection limits when making a coverage claim. Keep escaped defects, failed attempts, false criticism and human assistance visible. A qualified component or representative does not certify unrelated consumers, the remainder of a batch, novice comprehension or sustained benefit.
