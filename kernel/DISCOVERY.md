## Extrapolation
Trigger: explicit Extrapolation Protocol, maximum extrapolation or an equivalent broad discovery request. Ordinary tasks do not automatically become broad searches.
Recover the objective, baseline, current sources, prior outcomes/failures and parent constraints. Research relevant external/current methods when decision-changing.
Diverge across materially different lenses: assumption inversion, counterfactuals, abstraction, history, time propagation, constraint variation, negative space, contradiction, distant analogy and recombination. Preserve useful candidate batches before merging. Label OBSERVATION / INFERENCE / EXTRAPOLATION / SPECULATIVE_HYPOTHESIS.
Cross-pollinate mechanisms across domains and trace consequential secondary/tertiary effects with their assumptions. Rephrasings and echoes of one source are not independent convergence.
Challenge apparent novelty against recovered prior art, simpler explanations, contrary evidence and hidden dependencies. Do not confuse novelty with truth or delete a useful uncertain signal solely because it is uncertain.
After divergence, sift for evidence, surprise, explanatory value, consequence, falsifiability, applicability, reversibility and information value. Avoid fake precision and idea-count targets.
For a material survivor retain epistemic maturity, scope, consequence, incremental value, validation value, owner and next permitted treatment. Preserve falsifiers/resolving evidence where feasible. An interesting unresolved connection need not be forced into a fake binary test.
Capture through kernel/CAPTURE.md and kernel/LEARNING.md; detailed work stays in its domain. Return findings, useful questions/tests or bounded NO_NEW_SIGNAL. No execution or promotion follows automatically.

## Reach
Trigger: Reach on a target, Cross-Reach across specified objects, or System Reach. Deliberately revisit the possibility boundary of mature work with new evidence or new connections. A new publication is neither necessary nor sufficient.
Recover exact baseline/purpose and relevant source field, including quiet/older and unknown areas. Use bounded partitions for wide fields, then compare mechanisms across them; breadth does not require one giant context.
Reuse current Research findings and the Extrapolation process. Keep source observations, inference and speculation separate; qualify novelty relative to the baseline actually searched.
Apply kernel/CHECK.md to consequential candidates after sufficient divergence. Preserve original candidates and explain changed dispositions. Checkpoint scope, versions, coverage, candidate identities, findings and exact resume action before interruption.
Stop when required coverage/dispositions are adequate, distinct signal diminishes, the owner pauses, or a real source/resource boundary occurs. PARTIAL is honest; missing sources are not a clean negative result.
No ambient sweep, schedule, new project, paid endpoint or installed capability follows from this method. Reuse a separately authorized real observer only if it actually exists for this instance. Compare later usefulness/burden with ordinary Research; simplify/merge if the additional method adds no value.
