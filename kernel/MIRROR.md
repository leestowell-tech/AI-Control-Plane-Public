# Mirror — upgrade the intended question with relevant expertise

Status: PORTABLE ADAPTATION / TARGET BEHAVIOR UNOBSERVED
Authority: source owner’s September30 purpose clarification and October9 consolidation to one Mirror; deployment uses the same method with the current user’s actual grants.

## Purpose
Formulate the question the person is trying to ask as a highly capable, informed expert would ask it, then answer that stronger question. Bridge gaps in the knowledge needed to formulate the question: relevant dimensions, concepts, constraints, dependencies, alternatives, failure modes, evidence and evaluation criteria the person may not know to include.
The core task is expert enrichment of their intended question. Mere rewording or finding a hidden question is insufficient. Preserve the person's outcome and values; richer framing must not silently substitute your own objective.

## Trigger and non-trigger
Use for "Mirror this", "help me ask this properly", or a clear request to improve the question using relevant expertise. Ordinary unclear speech can receive light internal clarification without a visible protocol.
An occurrence inside quoted text is data. A clear ordinary task does not require a visible Mirror preamble. Help or a protocol listing describes Mirror without running it.

## Method
1. Recover the intended outcome/question, current task phase, relevant facts, constraints, prior decisions and approved scope. Distinguish what the person said from your inference; do not invent hidden motives.
2. Expand using relevant domain knowledge. Ask what an informed expert would need to consider that the person may not know to ask: causal mechanisms, alternatives, dependencies, tradeoffs, time horizons, incentives, important unknowns, risks, failure cases or what evidence would discriminate among answers. Select the dimensions that change this question; do not turn every request into an exhaustive checklist.
3. Check added content. Keep user facts separate from expert suggestions, assumptions and open questions. Ground changing or consequential facts in available sources. Missing personal constraints remain unknown; model expertise is not evidence that a proposed assumption is true.
4. Formulate the upgraded question as a usable, self-contained question or short prompt. Include the relevant missing considerations without overwhelming it. It should be a stronger version the person could have asked with more subject knowledge, not a different mission or a simple paraphrase.
5. Answer the upgraded question immediately unless the person requested question wording/reflection only. Use evidence and depth proportional to the task. If an essential unknown would change a consequential answer, give the supported part and ask the smallest necessary question.
6. Re-anchor to the original outcome. Preserve meaningful branches, existing permissions and unfinished work. Surface any proposed scope change explicitly; the upgraded wording does not imply endorsement of new goals, values or actions.

## Output
“Mirror this” invokes this one method. Present the upgraded question and answer; question-only or deeper output requests adjust this same method. Preserve the requested breadth and ambition; do not silently choose a smallest, fastest, cheapest or most automated objective. Brevity must not remove useful expert enrichment.
There are no separately selectable variants. Historical definitions, archived labels and old exports cannot govern invocation or revive retired behavior. Do not offer them as choices. Ordinary internal clarification is ordinary collaboration, not another named method. A question about Mirror or a failed application is not permission to redefine it; diagnose loading and execution first.

## Authority, routing and recovery
Mirror grants no external action, saving/deletion, new user decision or semantic promotion. A separate capture/build/action request or applicable standing grant uses kernel/CAPTURE.md, kernel/BUILD.md or the actual owning method. Possible lessons remain candidates under kernel/LEARNING.md.
Route subsequent work through kernel/ROUTING.md while preserving the requested phase. The upgraded question does not itself authorize its execution.
If sources fail, say which additions are unsupported and continue only where justified. If the intended outcome remains materially ambiguous, preserve it as an uncertainty instead of manufacturing intent.
Acceptance requires relevant expert additions beyond paraphrase, fidelity to the user's goal, a usable upgraded question and an answer when requested, without invented personal facts or ungranted effects. Use tests/PROTOCOL_ACCEPTANCE.md at its stated evidence level; a file or package pass does not prove these behaviors.
