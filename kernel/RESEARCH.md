# Research

Status: PORTABLE ADAPTATION / TARGET BEHAVIOR UNOBSERVED
Trigger: an explicit Research Protocol request or clear request for substantive investigation. Mere uncertainty, mention of research or starting another protocol does not launch a separate research mission. Build performs its own proportionate research.

## Recover and map
Recover the question, intended decision, original scope, relevant user/domain state, prior internal findings and failed/superseded approaches. Preserve the wider mission. Ask only for unrecoverable ambiguity that materially changes the assignment.
Build the field from the objective, prior evidence, authoritative field overviews, dependencies, lifecycle/failure cases and adjacent-domain probes. Use DEEP's lateral, abstraction and time lenses. For each material topic preserve question, relevance, required depth, sources, dependencies, findings and disposition. Mark REQUIRED / SHARED_VALIDATION / FUTURE / EXCLUDED with rationale. A generic topic list is only a starting hypothesis.

## Investigate
Give every required topic useful initial coverage before a favored branch consumes the run; deepen consequential uncertainties. Search with the field's vocabulary and alternatives, follow primary sources, related/citing work, competitors and failures. Use actual current official sources for changing product capability, primary evidence for empirical claims and dated first-person evidence for experience.
A title/abstract/listing is a lead. State inspected extent. Never imply a video was watched, paper read, repository tested or result reproduced when it was not. Seek lawful accessible originals when a source is blocked. Preserve missingness rather than fabricate an answer.

## Assess and synthesize
Each consequential conclusion retains source/location/version, observation versus author claim versus inference/hypothesis, contrary evidence, source dependence, limitations, applicability and decision implication.
Copies of one origin are not independent corroboration. Explain source disagreement in terms of populations, definitions, versions, incentives or unresolved conflict. Provide useful findings, first actions, dependencies, reusable methods and necessary tests—not only a link list. Research grants no implementation, outreach, paid access or semantic promotion.

## Coverage and stopping
Recheck original scope and the actual search trail. Look for omitted subjects/source classes, missed known anchors, weak vocabulary, unexamined contrary results and conclusions unsupported by inspected material. Required external investigation must actually occur if claimed.
A topic can be ANSWERED, UNCERTAIN, NEEDS_LOCAL_TEST, BLOCKED or EXCLUDED_WITH_REASON. A map is not an investigated field. Record PARTIAL when material executable branches remain or a real resource boundary interrupts. Reuse strong prior work instead of redoing it for activity.
Stop when coverage/dispositions and decision-critical evidence are adequate and additional varied searches have no specific plausible decision-changing target. One engine repeating the same results is not exhaustive coverage.
Checkpoint scope, sources, findings, unresolved work and exact resume action in the user's existing mission/domain record. Apply kernel/CAPTURE.md and kernel/LEARNING.md; verify receiving. Deliver concise conclusions, material uncertainty and the next justified action.
